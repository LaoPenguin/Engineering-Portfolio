# Zhenhuan Shao | Engineering Portfolio

Selected projects in digital IC design and computer architecture, with supporting research experience in photonics.

I am an undergraduate in Microelectronic Science and Engineering in the joint programme between the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China and the University of Glasgow. My interests centre on digital integrated circuits, processor architecture, and AI hardware.

This portfolio presents my contributions, design decisions, and the scope of verification for selected undergraduate projects.
